Are you looking for some alarms? Look no further!

Introducing _More SCP Alarm_, a mod that brings the thrilling alarm sounds from all your favorite SCP games! With this mod, you’ll experience immersive sound effects and even blocks inspired by the SCP universe. Whether you’re a fan of eerie sounds, intense atmospheres, or just want to elevate your SCP experience, this mod has it all.
